# ScholarCrafted, LLC: Executive Business Plan & Operational Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary

**ScholarCrafted, LLC** is a specialized academic consulting and manuscript architecture firm. Unlike high-volume proofreading agencies or transactional per-word editing platforms, ScholarCrafted provides high-touch intellectual partnership and structural editorial strategy for early-career scholars, postdocs, doctoral researchers, and university faculty in **Humanities, Social Sciences, and Non-STEM Disciplines**.

### The Problem
Early-career scholars face intense pressures to publish articles and produce first monographs to secure tenure, postdocs, or grant funding. However, academic writing at the doctoral level is often bloated, structurally unfocused, and poorly aligned with peer-reviewed journal requirements. Generic editing services focus merely on grammar, while supervisors often lack the time for developmental restructuring.

### The Solution
ScholarCrafted fills this critical gap by delivering high-impact **Manuscript Architecture and Thesis-to-Journal Conversions**. We do not sell micro-services or word counts; we sell peer-review readiness and narrative coherence through structured diagnostics and end-to-end consulting.

## 3. Pricing, Entry Points & Scope Architecture

### A. The 2 Universal Website Entry Points

Visitors to the site do not select from a complex dropdown list of 10 micro-services. They choose between **two low-friction entry pathways**:

1. **Option 1: Express Structural Diagnostic ($29 Flat Fee)**
   * **Target Client:** Hesitant leads, budget-conscious PhDs, or scholars needing rapid validation before a deadline.
   * **Process:** Client uploads a sample file (e.g., 10-page chapter, abstract, or proposal).
   * **Deliverable:** 2-page written **Argument, Structure & AI/Integrity Audit** + Optional 15-Minute Diagnostic Call.
   * **Conversion Goal:** Builds instant trust and establishes intellectual authority, converting the lead into a full Track 1 or Track 2 project package.

2. **Option 2: Direct Project Scope & Custom Quote**
   * **Target Client:** Scholars with active, substantial projects ready for full developmental partnership.
   * **Process:** Client completes a short 3-question intake form (Project Type, Current Stage/Word Count, Deadline).
   * **Deliverable:** Direct booking link for a **15-Minute Scope & Consultation Call**, followed by an **Integrated Milestone Proposal**.

## 5. Marketing & Client Acquisition Strategy

ScholarCrafted operates three parallel acquisition engines:

### Engine A: Borrowed-Trust Referral System (Micah Partnership)
* **Strategy:** Position senior collaborators (like Micah) as academic advocates sharing useful assets, not commercial sales reps.
* **Assets Provided:** 1-page PDF guide (*From Dissertation to Peer-Reviewed Article: A Structural Framework*) and clean introduction email templates.
* **Conflict-of-Interest Shield:** All case studies based on collaborative work are presented through **Anonymized Structural Breakdowns** (focusing on structural transformation rather than personal/financial ties).

### Engine B: The LinkedIn Comment-Section Strategy
* **Strategy:** Demonstrate intellectual discernment in high-density academic spaces where buyers spend time.
* **Execution:** 5–10 insightful comments per week on posts by faculty, research centers, and academic authors discussing research, publishing, and project friction.
* **Funnel Path:** Insightful Comment $\rightarrow$ Profile Visit $\rightarrow$ Evidence/Testimonial $\rightarrow$ $29 Diagnostic or Scope Inquiry.

### Engine C: Consultation Call & Proposal Conversion
* **Consultation Framework:** 
  1. *Understand the project* ("What are you working on?")
  2. *Find the friction* ("What's proving most difficult?")
  3. *Understand the stakes* ("Is there a deadline?")
  4. *Reflect what you heard* ("It sounds like the real issue is X, not Y.")
  5. *Recommend defined next step* (Tailored flat-fee proposal with 50/50 payment split).
