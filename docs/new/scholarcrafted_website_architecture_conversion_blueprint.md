# ScholarCrafted: Website Architecture & Conversion Blueprint

## 1. The Psychological Shift: Why the New Flow Converts Higher

### The Old Model (Transactional / E-Commerce Shopping Cart)
* **What it did:** Presented 4 dropdown categories, 3 competing tier options (*Advanced*, *Premium*, *Scientific Pro*), and a live per-word calculator ($0.0425/word, $0.065/word, $0.095/word).
* **The Psychological Flaw:** 
  1. **Positioned you as a commodity:** Academics evaluated you like a generic proofreading website or Fiverr seller.
  2. **Decision Paralysis:** The buyer had to self-diagnose whether they needed "Advanced Line Editing" or "Scientific Pro," creating anxiety that they might buy the wrong option or get nickel-and-dimed later.
  3. **High Price Sensitivity:** Calculating $325 live on a slider makes academics hesitate and compare your prices against free AI tools or cheap freelancers.

### The New Model (Consultative Outcome Architecture)
* **What it does:** Replaces messy pricing sliders and competing tiers with **2 Clear Outcome Tracks** (Journal Articles vs. Book Manuscripts) and **2 Low-Friction Entry Pathways** ($29 Express Diagnostic vs. Custom Scope Request).
* **The Psychological Advantage:**
  1. **Positioned as an Intellectual Partner:** You sell peer-review readiness, structural narrative coherence, and publisher acceptance.
  2. **Zero Self-Diagnosis Needed:** The client simply selects what they are working on (an article or a book).
  3. **Preserves All Editorial Details:** All your rich capabilities (Track Changes, APA/Chicago style manuals, journal compliance, Editorial Certificates) are showcased as *quality guarantees* within the tracks, rather than confusing add-on choices.

## 4. Track Landing Pages: Keeping Your Rich Details

You do **not** lose any of your technical details (Track Changes, formatting standards, Editorial Certificates, etc.). You simply organize them cleanly under each track landing page as **Standard Quality Commitments**.

### Track A Page Layout (Thesis-to-Journal & Paper Prep)

#### 1. Hero Section
* **Headline:** Turn Your Thesis Chapters or Research Drafts into Peer-Reviewed Journal Articles.
* **Subheadline:** Stop getting desk-rejected for structural bloat. ScholarCrafted provides structural extraction, substantive developmental editing, and complete target journal compliance.

#### 2. The 3-Phase Conversion Process
Show clients exactly how their manuscript moves through your system:
* **Phase 1: Structural Extraction & Argument Mapping** — We isolate your core contribution, condense 20,000-word dissertation chapters into sharp 7,000-word article logic, and align your theoretical hook.
* **Phase 2: Scholarly Rigor & Style Refinement** — Substantive line editing, tone elevation, logical pacing, and discipline-specific vocabulary verification.
* **Phase 3: Technical & Journal Compliance** — Complete formatting to target journal specifications (APA 7th, Chicago, IEEE, MLA), LaTeX/Typst document engineering, cover letter drafting, and Editorial Compliance Certification.

#### 3. Standard Quality Guarantees (Where your details live!)
Every Track A project automatically includes:
* 100% Track Changes delivery in Microsoft Word (or LaTeX source files).
* Strict Zero Ghostwriting & Academic Integrity Pledge.
* Discipline-specific editorial matching.
* Official Editorial Compliance & Language Certificate for journal submission.

## 5. The Post-Lead Conversion Workflow

Here is how a prospect moves from visiting your site to becoming a paying client:

```
[ Visitor Lands on Site ]
         │
         ├───► Clicks "$29 Diagnostic" ──► Uploads sample file ──► Receives Audit + Debrief Call ──► Converts to Track Package
         │
         └───► Clicks "Request Scope" ──► Fills 3-Question Form ──► 15-Min Consultation Call ──► Receives Custom Milestone Proposal
```

### The Proposal Structure (What you send after the call):
Instead of asking them to calculate words, you send a single professional proposal document with **One Total Project Fee** broken into milestones:

* **Project Scope:** *Thesis-to-Journal Conversion (Target: Journal of Academic History)*
* **Milestone 1 (50% Deposit):** Structural Extraction & Argument Architecture Audit.
* **Milestone 2:** Substantive Line Editing, Logic Refinement & Integrity Check.
* **Milestone 3 (50% Final Payment):** Target Journal Template Formatting (LaTeX/Word), Cover Letter & Final Delivery.
* **Total Investment:** $1,400 *(Terms: $700 upfront / $700 upon delivery)*.
