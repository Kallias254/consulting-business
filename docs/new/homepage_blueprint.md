# ScholarCrafted: Redesigned Homepage Architecture & Copy Blueprint

## 1. Executive Assessment: What to Keep vs. What to Replace

### KEEP (High Conversion Power):
* **The 8 Situation Cards ("Find Your Starting Point"):** Brilliant UX. Academics recognize their exact current situation ("defense in 3 weeks," "R&R response," "thesis to article").
* **Social Proof & Metrics Bar (500+ Faculty, 100% Tracked Changes, Top Presses):** Essential for establishing institutional credibility.
* **Specialist Team Profiles & Testimonials:** Builds trust by showing real doctoral-level editorial partners, not automated AI tools.
* **Ethics & Confidentiality Highlights:** Keeps PhDs and faculty reassured regarding university compliance and COPE standards.

### REPLACE / EVOLVE (Friction Points):
* **Live Per-Word Calculators & 3 Competing Tiers ($0.0425, $0.065, $0.095/word):** Replaced with **2 Outcome Tracks** and the **$29 Express Diagnostic**. (Prevents price shopping against cheap freelancers).
* **Shopping Cart Dropdowns (4 Service Categories):** Replaced with a unified 2-pathway CTA (Express Diagnostic vs. Custom Scope Request).
