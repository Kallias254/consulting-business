# ScholarCrafted: End-to-End Client Relationship & Psychological Onboarding Workflow

## 1. Deconstructing the Call: Necessity, Hosting Roles, and Asynchronous Alternatives

### A. Is the Call Mandatory for Both Entry Paths?
**No, the call is NOT mandatory in either path.** It is framed as an **optional high-value strategic debrief** or **scoping consultation**. 

#### The Academic Psychology:
Academics (especially introverted PhD candidates and busy faculty) fall into two communication camps:
1. **The Collaborative Scholar:** Prefers talking through their thesis/book ideas to test your intellectual depth.
2. **The Asynchronous Scholar:** Prefers written feedback due to schedule constraints, anxiety about defending their work live, or time zone differences.

Forcing a mandatory call creates friction for Camp #2. Offering an optional 15-minute debrief ensures zero lost conversions.

---
