# 27: Provocative Social Media Marketing Strategy & Visual Flyer Blueprints

## 1. Executive Strategy & Objective

This document formalizes the organic social media strategy, channel architecture, and provocative visual flyer campaigns for **ScholarCrafted**. 

### Strategic Positioning: The Independent Academic Counterweight
Doctoral scholars and early-career researchers are trapped between an extractive publishing industry (Elsevier, Springer, Taylor & Francis) and absentee university mentorship. They are overwhelmed by contradictory committee feedback, paranoid about uncalibrated AI detectors, and paralyzed by the final stages of writing.

ScholarCrafted positions itself not as a generic SaaS tool or corporate vendor, but as **an independent, faculty-level ally** providing rigorous structural triage, citation forensics, and press-ready execution.

## 3. The 4-Beat Post Caption Framework

When publishing these flyers, pair the visual asset with a text post adhering to the following structure:

1. **The Uncomfortable Hook**: State an unspoken academic truth that triggers immediate recognition.
2. **The Shared Agony**: Validate the scholar's emotional exhaustion without sounding patronizing.
3. **The Line in the Sand (Our Stance)**: Contrast commoditized AI shortcuts or corporate publishing traps with authentic faculty rigor.
4. **The Frictionless Action**: Direct the reader to the specific service pillar, calculator, or diagnostic intake.
