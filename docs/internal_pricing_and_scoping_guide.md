#### Word Count Quoting Formula:
$$\text{Project Fee} = \text{Manuscript Word Count} \times \text{Tier Rate}$$
*Example (7,500-word Article):*
* Advanced: $7,500 \times 0.0425 = \$318.75$ (Round to: **$320**)
* Premium: $7,500 \times 0.0650 = \$487.50$ (Round to: **$490**)
* Scientific Pro: $7,500 \times 0.0950 = \$712.50$ (Round to: **$715**)

*Example (15,000-word Dissertation Chapters):*
* Advanced: $15,000 \times 0.0425 = \$637.50$ (Round to: **$640**)
* Premium: $15,000 \times 0.0650 = \$975.00$ (Round to: **$975**)
* Scientific Pro: $15,000 \times 0.0950 = \$1,425.00$ (Round to: **$1,425**)

*Example (80,000-word Full Monograph / Book):*

## 3. The Consultative Quoting Algorithm (How to Scope Inbound Leads)

When an inbound enquiry or $29 diagnostic arrives, follow this standardized 4-step algorithm to construct the client's proposal.

```
[ Inbound Inquiry: Word Count + Current Stage + Target Journal/Press ]
                                  │
                                  ▼
      Step 1: Determine Track (Track A: Short-Form vs. Track B: Long-Form)
                                  │
                                  ▼
      Step 2: Calculate Base Word Rate + Add-On Modules
                                  │
                                  ▼
      Step 3: Deduct $29 Diagnostic Credit (if applicable)
                                  │
                                  ▼
      Step 4: Package into 3 Milestone Phases with 50/50 Payment Terms
```

### Milestone Proposal Templates

#### Proposal Example 1: Track A (Thesis-to-Journal Article Conversion)
* **Client Scenario:** PhD candidate converting a 14,000-word dissertation chapter into an 8,000-word article for an Elsevier or Routledge journal.
* **Internal Calculation:**
  * Substantive Extraction & Editing (Premium rate on 8,000 target words): $8,000 \times 0.065 = \$520$
  * Extraction & Structural Chapter Condensation Fee: $\$380$
  * Target Journal Packaging & Cover Letter (Journal Pro module): $\$350$
  * Total Gross Value: $\$1,250$
  * Minus $29 Diagnostic Credit: $-\$29$
  * **Final Quoted Project Fee:** **$1,220 flat**
* **Milestone Structure in Proposal:**
  * *Phase 1 (50% Deposit = $610):* Structural Extraction, Logic Architecture & Argument Mapping.
  * *Phase 2:* Substantive Line Editing, Academic Tone & Citation Cross-Audit.
  * *Phase 3 (50% Balance = $610):* Target Journal Formatting (LaTeX/Word), Cover Letter & Compliance Certificate.

#### Proposal Example 2: Track B (Monograph & Book Architecture)
* **Client Scenario:** Assistant Professor preparing their first monograph (75,000 words) for Oxford University Press.
* **Internal Calculation:**
  * Full Developmental Line Edit: $75,000 \times 0.0425 = \$3,187.50$
  * Publisher Proposal Polish (Stage 02): $\$480$
  * Press Indexing Allocation (approx. 250 pages $\times$ $3): $\$750$
  * **Total Quoted Project Bundle:** **$3,800 flat**
* **Milestone Structure in Proposal:**
  * *Phase 1 (50% Deposit = $1,900):* Multi-Chapter Narrative Roadmap & Proposal Submission.
  * *Phase 2:* Full Developmental Line Edit & Citation Verification.
  * *Phase 3 (50% Balance = $1,900):* Galley Proof Mark-Up & Back-of-Book Indexing.
